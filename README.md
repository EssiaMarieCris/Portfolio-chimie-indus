# Industrial Chemistry & Cheminformatics Portfolio

Professional computational portfolio showcasing applications in Process Analytical Technology (PAT), predictive cheminformatics, and formulation optimization for pharmaceutical and chemical industries.

---

## Portfolio Modules

### 1. `pharma-chemometrics/` (Completed)
- **Focus**: Process Analytical Technology (PAT) & Spectral Preprocessing.
- **Workflow**: Simulated Near-Infrared (NIR) spectroscopy, Standard Normal Variate (SNV) correction, and Principal Component Analysis (PCA) for raw material discrimination (APIs vs. Excipients).
- **Standards**: Aligned with ICH Q8 guidelines.

### 2. `deeptox-toxicity-prediction/` (In Progress)
- **Focus**: Quantitative Structure-Toxicity Relationship (QSTR) Modeling.
- **Workflow**: Molecular descriptor generation and supervised machine learning for in silico chemical safety screening.

### 3. `formulation-optimization/` (Planned)
- **Focus**: Design of Experiments (DoE) & Quality by Design (QbD).
- **Workflow**: Response Surface Methodology (RSM) to optimize chemical formulations and process parameters.

---

## Environment & Reproducibility
- **Platform**: Python 3.10+ (Anaconda environment: `deeptox`)
- **Key Libraries**: `numpy`, `scipy`, `scikit-learn`, `matplotlib`, `pandas`
